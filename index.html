Welcome to my website!

I am a PhD candidate at the Department of Economics at the University of Zurich. My research focuses on Behavioral and Microeconomic Theory, with particular emphasis on Persuasion and Information Design.

I am currently visiting the Economics Department at Princeton University.

I hold a MSc and a BSc in Economics and Social Sciences from Bocconi University in Milan, Italy.
